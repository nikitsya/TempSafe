## What to observe

- Did the participant understand the current temperature and status?
- Did they assume that a current Normal status meant nothing had happened earlier?
- Did they find the previous event without help?
- Could they identify the event date, time, estimated duration and peak temperature?
- Was any information unclear or missing?
